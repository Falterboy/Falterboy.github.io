ABOUT FALTERBOY

Falterboy is the musical alter-ego of Jeremy “Buff” Schantz — a Michigan-born, fantasy-loving, D&D-rolling kid who grew up to become a Tampa Bay songwriter and frontman with a guitar, a story, and a pair of very worn-in Chuck Taylors.

His music combines emotionally heavy, sharply honest lyrics with upbeat indie rock, pop-punk energy, and a strong sense of melody. The result is music that can make you move while quietly punching you in the feelings.

Falterboy's songs explore nostalgia, heartbreak, procrastination, anxiety, resilience, and the strange ways people learn to keep moving forward. Live, Schantz brings a raw, energetic performance built around connection, storytelling, and a healthy dose of humor.
